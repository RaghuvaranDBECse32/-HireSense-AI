Senior Backend Engineer (Python / Cloud)

About the Role:
We are seeking an experienced Backend Engineer to join our core platform engineering team. You will lead the architecture and deployment of high-throughput backend services powering our SaaS infrastructure.

Requirements:
- 3+ years of professional backend software development experience.
- Strong proficiency in Python, with hands-on experience using modern asynchronous frameworks (FastAPI or asyncio).
- Production experience with relational databases (PostgreSQL preferred) and database query optimization.
- Solid understanding of containerization (Docker) and CI/CD pipelines.
- Experience building RESTful APIs and microservice architectures.

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience deploying and managing workloads on AWS (ECS, EKS, Lambda, S3).
- Hands-on knowledge of Kubernetes cluster management and Helm charts.
- Familiarity with event-driven architectures using Apache Kafka or RabbitMQ.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related technical discipline.

Responsibilities:
- Design, build, and deploy resilient backend microservices.
- Optimize database schemas and queries for low-latency transaction processing.
- Collaborate with frontend engineers and product managers to deliver customer-facing features.
- Participate in code reviews and mentor junior engineering team members.
